King Legends: Survivor

Privacy Policy

King Legends: Survivor · Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains what data the King Legends: Survivor mobile game for iOS and Android ("the Game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The Game is developed by Berkay Hapcioglu, an individual developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). We are the data controller under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact: hello@onbers.com

1. No account, no personal profile

You can play without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or contacts. Your game progress is stored on your device.

Cloud save (optional, when available). If you turn on cloud save, we store your game progress and an account identifier (an anonymous ID, or the ID from Sign in with Apple or Google sign-in) on Google Firebase servers so you can continue on another device. We do not receive your Apple or Google password, and with Sign in with Apple you can hide your e-mail address. Cloud saves are kept until you delete them or your account.

2. Data collected automatically

The Game and the services in section 4 collect the following automatically, through software components (SDKs) built into the app:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, time zone, app version.
  • Approximate location: country or region derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Identifiers: the identifier for vendors (IDFV), an app installation ID and, only if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt, the advertising identifier (IDFA). On Android: the Google advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings.
  • Gameplay and usage data: sessions, levels reached, in-game choices, ads viewed or tapped, and purchases made (product and price; your payment details stay with Apple or Google and never reach us).
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data.

5. International transfers

These services may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses under the GDPR and the transfer rules of Article 9 of the KVKK.

6. Retention

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days. Advertising partners keep data under their own policies. Game data on your device is deleted when you delete the Game.

7. Your choices

  • Tracking: allow or deny tracking in Apple's prompt, and change it any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Cloud save: delete your cloud save and account at any time from Settings in the Game.
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): reopen the consent screen from Settings > Privacy choices in the Game. In these regions, analytics also follows the choice you make there.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of Türkiye have the rights listed in Article 11 of the KVKK. Because there are no accounts, please include the Support ID shown on the Game's Settings screen when you write to hello@onbers.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We work with service providers that use industry-standard security measures. No method of transfer or storage is completely secure.

11. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date at the top. We will also announce significant changes in the Game.
