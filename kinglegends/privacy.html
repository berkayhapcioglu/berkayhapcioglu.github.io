King Legends: Survivor

Privacy Policy

King Legends: Survivor · Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains what data the King Legends: Survivor mobile game for iOS and Android ("the Game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The Game is developed by Berkay Hapcioglu, an individual developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). We are the data controller under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact: hello@onbers.com

1. Playing without an account

You can play without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, phone number or contacts. Your game progress is stored on your device. Signing in for cloud save is optional (see below).

Leaderboards and player profiles (signed-in players only). If you sign in with Apple or Google, your scores (Endless March depth for the week and all time, and your furthest campaign stage), your in-game name and your platform (iOS, Android or Steam) appear on the Game's public leaderboards, where any player can see them. Tapping a name opens that player's public profile: their character's look (costume, ruler and city theme), Battle Power and combat stats, equipped gear, pet, progress (furthest stage, Endless March depth, dynasty generation, runs played and bosses defeated), platform, game version and when the profile was last updated. You can type your own in-game name or keep a preset one. Typed names are checked by an automatic word filter, both in the Game and on our server, and each name can belong to only one player (to check this we store a simplified lowercase form of each name). Signed-in players can report a name in the Game (guests can e-mail us). A report stores the reporting and the reported player's account IDs, the reported name, the reason and the time, and only we can read it. If a name breaks the rules we replace it with a preset name and may stop that account from typing a new name for a while or permanently; the profile then also stores when the name was reset and when the lock ends. Reports are deleted once we have handled them. All of this is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany) under your account ID, never with your real name or e-mail address. Guests are not listed and have no public profile. Deleting your account in Settings > Account > Delete account also removes your leaderboard entries, your profile and your name.

Clans, friends, chat and Throne Duels (signed-in players only). These features need an Apple or Google sign-in. What they store is kept in Google Cloud Firestore in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany) under your account ID, never with your real name or e-mail address.

  • Clans: the clan's name, description, emblem, language and settings; for each member the in-game name, role, furthest stage, level, join date and weekly contribution, donation and Clan Boss points; invitations and join requests; the clan's weekly ranking and Clan Boss damage. Any player can see clan pages and member lists.
  • Friends: your 8-character player code (other players can use it to add you or invite you to a clan), friend requests, your friends list and daily gifts. Only you see your friends list; players see each other's name on requests and gifts.
  • Chat: the messages you write in clan chat (seen by your clan's members) and in the Turkish or English global chat (seen by signed-in players), with your in-game name, look, clan tag and the time. Messages pass the same word filter as names and are limited to 140 characters. Clan chat messages are deleted after about 14 days and global chat messages after about 2 days; you, clan officers and we can delete them earlier.
  • Reports, blocks and mutes: you can report a message or block a player in the Game. Blocks are kept on your device. A report stores your and the reported player's account IDs, the message, the room, the reason and the time, and only we can read it. We review reports within 24 hours, delete messages that break the rules and can stop an account from chatting for a day, a week or permanently. Reports are deleted once we have handled them.
  • Throne Duels: a weekly entry with your in-game name, look, ruler, Battle Power, duel rating, wins and losses. Signed-in players can see it in the duel ranking and fight a computer-controlled shadow of your ruler; you lose nothing when they do.

Deleting your account also takes you out of your clan (leadership passes to another member, and a clan with no one left is removed) and deletes your messages, invitations, requests, player code, friendships, gifts and duel entries.

Subscriptions. Royal Privilege is an auto-renewing monthly subscription sold and managed by Apple. We receive only the product, its status and its expiry date, never your payment details. You can cancel it any time in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

Cloud save (optional, when available). If you choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, we create an account for you in Google Firebase Authentication and keep your save in Google Cloud Firestore in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany), so you can continue on another device. The save contains your game progress, your stage and dynasty, the time of the save and your device model. With Sign in with Apple we do not request your name or e-mail address. With Google sign-in, Google shares your e-mail address with us and it is stored with your account. We never receive your Apple or Google password. Your account and cloud save are kept until you delete them in the Game under Settings > Account > Delete account, which deletes both.

2. Data collected automatically

The Game and the services in section 4 collect the following automatically, through software components (SDKs) built into the app:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, time zone, app version.
  • Approximate location: country or region derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Identifiers: the identifier for vendors (IDFV), an app installation ID and, only if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt, the advertising identifier (IDFA). On Android: the Google advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings.
  • Gameplay and usage data: sessions, levels reached, in-game choices, ads viewed or tapped, and purchases made (product and price; your payment details stay with Apple or Google and never reach us).
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data.

Gameplay statistics and surveys. To see where players get stuck and to balance the Game, the Game sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase and to our own database in Google Cloud Firestore in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany): progress and battle results, session times, purchases and ads viewed, device model, operating system and app version, language and region setting, and your answers to optional in-game surveys. To store them, the Game signs your installation in to Firebase Authentication anonymously. The events are linked only to a random anonymous ID; the Support ID shown in the Game lets us find and delete your data. They do not include your name, e-mail address, precise location or advertising identifier, and they are not used for tracking or personalized ads. You can turn them off at any time in the Game under Settings > Privacy > Share gameplay data.

5. International transfers

These services may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses under the GDPR and the transfer rules of Article 9 of the KVKK.

6. Retention

Analytics data in Google Analytics is kept for up to 14 months, gameplay statistics in our database for up to 24 months, and crash reports for up to 90 days. Advertising partners keep data under their own policies. Game data on your device is deleted when you delete the Game.

7. Your choices

  • Tracking: allow or deny tracking in Apple's prompt, and change it any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Cloud save: delete your account and cloud save at any time in the Game under Settings > Account > Delete account.
  • Gameplay statistics: turn them off in the Game under Settings > Privacy > Share gameplay data. To have statistics already sent deleted, e-mail us your Support ID.
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): reopen the consent screen from Settings > Privacy choices in the Game. In these regions, analytics also follows the choice you make there.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of Türkiye have the rights listed in Article 11 of the KVKK. If you play without an account, please include the Support ID shown on the Game's Settings screen when you write to hello@onbers.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We work with service providers that use industry-standard security measures. No method of transfer or storage is completely secure.

11. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date at the top. We will also announce significant changes in the Game.
