English

I bought No Ads but still see ads. Open Settings in the Game and tap Restore Purchases. Rewarded ads stay available because they are always optional.

I lost my progress. Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the Game deletes it. Contact us with your Support ID and we will try to help.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices? Settings > Privacy choices in the Game, and iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I ask for my data to be deleted? E-mail us with your Support ID.
